# Minecraft Teleportation Staffs Mod

## Overview
In a world filled with perils and mysteries, two ancient staffs have resurfaced, each imbued with the power to alter the very fabric of space itself. These mystical tools grant their wielder the ability to teleport to the spot their gaze falls upon, offering both escape from danger and access to otherwise unreachable places.

## Features
This mod introduces two unique teleportation staffs into your Minecraft world, each with its own characteristics:

1. **Wooden Staff**  
   Crafted from humble wood, this staff is simple to forge, making it the perfect tool for any adventurer.  
   - **Teleportation Range**: 7 blocks ahead  
   - **Durability**: Breaks after a few uses  
   - **Crafting**: Easy to craft with basic materials

2. **Mystic Staff**  
   A relic of rare components and powerful magic, this staff requires the gathering of elusive materials to craft, but it rewards the player with enhanced powers.  
   - **Teleportation Range**: 12 blocks ahead  
   - **Durability**: Stronger and lasts longer  
   - **Crafting**: Requires rare materials scattered across the lands

## Installation
1. Download the mod from GitHub or CurseForge.
2. Place the mod file into the "mods" folder in your Minecraft directory.
3. Launch Minecraft with the required Forge or Fabric mod loader.
4. Enjoy the power of teleportation!

## How to Use
1. Equip the **Wooden Staff** or **Mystic Staff**.
2. Point your cursor in the direction you wish to teleport.
3. Right-click to teleport to the location your gaze falls upon.
   
   - **Wooden Staff** will teleport you up to **7 blocks**.
   - **Mystic Staff** will teleport you up to **12 blocks**.

## Crafting Recipes
- **Wooden Staff**: A simple crafting recipe involving basic wood and sticks.
- **Mystic Staff**: Requires rare materials found across the world, including mystical stones and rare gems.

## Configuration
The mod comes with a configurable option for the teleportation distances and durability of the staffs, allowing for customization to fit your gameplay.

## Compatibility
This mod is compatible with Minecraft 1.XX+ and works best with the latest version of **Forge** or **Fabric** mod loaders.
